## A. Cross-cutting findings (affect both papers)

### X1. CRITICAL, VERIFIED: the "level of distribution θ_W = 5/8 over cascade moduli 3^K" is either trivial for every exponent below 1 or beyond GRH. In neither case is 5/8 forced.
- **Where:** p02:31 "the level-of-distribution result θ_W = 5/8 proved in the companion papers"; p02:121, 139 ("level-of-distribution breakthrough"); p03:74–77, 143–144, 162–165, 244–247, 400–405, 423–426, 626–628, 645–647 ("as rigorous as any analytic number theory").
- **What the companion papers actually state** (p04 Thm 1.1 at p04.tex:115–127; p05 Thm 5.7 at p05.tex:488–497):
  Σ_{K≥1, 3^K ≤ x^{5/8−ε}} max_{(a,3)=1} |ψ(x;3^K,a) − x/φ(3^K)| ≪ x/(log x)^A.
- **Why the statement is trivial.** The family has only about log x members, so it holds with 5/8 replaced by any θ < 1:
  - Split at 3^K = (log x)^{A+2}.
  - Small moduli: Siegel–Walfisz gives each term ≪ x/(log x)^{A+1}. There are only O(log log x) such K. The only real character mod 3^K is χ_{−3} (fact 6), so the estimate is even effective.
  - Large moduli: the trivial bound gives |ψ(x;q,a) − x/φ(q)| ≤ (x/q + 1)·log x + 3x/(2q) ≪ x·log x/q.
  - The sum over 3^K > (log x)^{A+2} is a geometric series, ≪ x/(log x)^{A+1}.
  - So the stated "theorem" needs no Eisenstein filtration, no Kloosterman input, no "Condition W3" and no Watt's theorem, and "uniqueness of 5/8" (p04 Thm 5.1) is false.
- **Numerical check (`cascade_trivial.py`, x = 10^8).** The cumulative sum, as a fraction of x, is 2.89e-4 up to θ ≈ 0.596 and 2.99e-4 up to θ ≈ 0.954. Moduli beyond x^{5/8} add about 3% to the sum. Nothing changes at θ = 5/8.
- **The other reading is unproved.** If "level 5/8" is meant per modulus (error = o(main term) for each 3^K ≤ x^{5/8}), it exceeds what GRH gives (q ≤ x^{1/2−ε}). The known prime-power-modulus exponents are 3/8, 2/5, 5/12 and about 0.47 (fact 5). That would be an extraordinary claim with no valid proof.
- **The family is useless for GPY.** Moduli 3^K with K ≥ 2 are not squarefree. GPY, Maynard and Tao sieve weights λ_d are supported on squarefree d, so these moduli never occur in the GPY input. "Extending θ_W = 5/8 to a moduli family broad enough to serve as GPY input" (p03:427–431) therefore has nothing to extend.
- **Correction:** Delete every claim of a "proved"/"established" level 5/8 for 3^K. At most state the trivial fact above, which holds for every θ < 1.
- **Propagates:** facts 5, 6 and 7.

### X2. CRITICAL, VERIFIED: the counting theorem behind 5/8 = (2^3 − 3)/2^3 is not a theorem (fact 2), and the claimed link to the mod-3 Pascal fractal is false.
- **Where:** p02:119 "the Sierpiński void pattern at scale 3^K matches exactly the count of invalid configurations in a K-level cascade sieve … exactly k invalid configurations out of 2^k total … for k=3 yields 5/8"; p03:140–142 ("derives the universal formula … from combinatorial first principles"); p03:407–412 ("This count traces to the mod-3 Sierpiński structure of Khayyam's Triangle").
- **Counts from `p02_pascal.py`.** Over the first 3^K rows mod 3:

  | K | zero entries | nonzero entries | survivor density | (2^K − K)/2^K |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | 1 | 0 | 6 | 1 | 1/2 |
  | 2 | 9 | 36 | 4/5 | 1/2 |
  | 3 | 162 | 216 | 4/7 ≈ 0.571 | 5/8 |
  | 4 | 2025 | 1296 | 16/41 | 3/4 |
  | 5 | 21870 | 7776 | 16/61 | 27/32 |

  - The voids number 3^K(3^K+1)/2 − 6^K, never K.
  - The survivor density tends to 0, while (2^K − K)/2^K tends to 1: opposite trends.
  - The per-digit survival ratio under Lucas is 6/9 = 2/3 (three states per base-3 digit, not binary configurations), giving (2/3)^K over the full square.
  - No statistic of the triangle equals 2^K − K.
- **The cited proof is wrong too.** The source of the claim (p05 Prop 2.3) asserts "nonzero entries replicate at each scale by a factor of 3". The factor is 6 (see P2).
- **The induction fails (fact 2):** one failing level invalidates 2^{k−1} configurations, and the induction gives 2(k−1) + 1 invalid configurations, not k.
- **Correction:** Remove the "combinatorial ancestor" and "traces to" claims, and state that 2^k − k is a stipulation.

### X3. CRITICAL, VERIFIED: twin primes carry no 3-adic information beyond p ≡ 2 (mod 3), so "k = 3 levels" is unfounded (fact 3).
- **Where:** p03:395–398 "induces a natural filtration on Z[ω]/(3^K) with exactly three independent levels"; p03:382–386 "forces attention to … q = 3^K"; p03:415–416 "All five consequences are forced by the single constitutional fact …"; p02:119 ("K-level cascade sieve").
- **Evidence from `p03_twin_sg.py`:**
  - Every lift of 2 (mod 3) to mod 9, 27 or 81 keeps both p and p+2 coprime to 3: the "killing lifts" list is empty in every case.
  - Empirically, the 440,311 twin primes below 10^8 are uniform over the lifts. The ratio to the mean is 0.998–1.003 mod 9 (χ² = 1.8, df 2), 0.994–1.008 mod 27 (χ² = 8.3, df 8) and 0.988–1.014 mod 81 (χ² = 23.5, df 26).
  - So the conditions at 9 and 27 are not constraints at all, and by criterion (C2) the depth is 1.
- **The filtration has 2K levels, not 3.** The λ-adic filtration of Z[ω]/(3^K) has 2K steps, since (3^K) = (1−ω)^{2K} and each quotient is F_3. The companion paper p05 Prop 5.3 says exactly this. p04 Prop 2.4 then selects "j = 2, 4, 6" arbitrarily to obtain "three".
- **Correction:** k for twin primes cannot be 3 on these grounds. Drop "exactly three independent levels".

### X4. CRITICAL, VERIFIED: a level of distribution θ > 1/2 does NOT yield twin primes.
- **Where:** p03:225–228 "would imply … bounded gaps, and in particular—via a refinement—would yield infinitely many twin primes"; p03:421–423 "GPY requires θ > 1/2 … to conclude twin prime infinitude"; p03:176–180 (frames the GPY input as "the principal open step").
- **What GPY and its successors actually give:**
  - GPY (Ann. Math. 170, 2009): θ > 1/2 gives bounded gaps. Level 1 (Elliott–Halberstam) gives only gaps ≤ 16.
  - Maynard: EH gives ≤ 12.
  - Polymath8b: GEH gives ≤ 6.
  - The parity problem (Selberg) blocks gap 2 for these sieve methods at any level.
  - Maynard–Tao obtain bounded gaps with θ = 1/2 (indeed any θ > 0). The "θ = 1/2 barrier" is therefore not what separates bounded gaps from twin primes.
- **Consequence:** even a complete version of the programme's "open step" would not prove twin primes.
- **Correction:** Remove the implication and state the parity barrier.

## B. p02: Khayyam's Triangle

### P1. MAJOR, VERIFIED: the Hausdorff dimension formula is wrong.
- **Where:** p02:105–111 (Prop 4.2) "Hausdorff dimension d = log(p+1)/log p. For p=3 this gives d = log 4 / log 3 ≈ 1.262".
- **Correct values:** The number of nonzero entries mod p in the first p^n rows is (p(p+1)/2)^n, so d = log(p(p+1)/2)/log p. Computed for p = 2, 3, 5, 7 and n ≤ 4 (`p02_pascal.py`: p = 3 gives 6, 36, 216, 1296 = 6^n, not 4^n).

  | p | correct d | paper's formula |
  |---|---|---|
  | 2 | 1.58496 | 1.58496 (coincidence) |
  | 3 | log 6/log 3 = 1.63093 | 1.26186 |
  | 5 | 1.68261 | 1.11328 |
  | 7 | 1.71241 | 1.06862 |

- The paper's formula even decreases towards 1 as p grows. The true dimension increases towards 2.
- **The paper's own sources contradict it.** Reiter (Fibonacci Quart. 31(2) 1993, Theorem 2) gives ln[p(p+1)/2]/ln p, and Holte (Fib. Quart. 2006) attributes the same formula to Wolfram 1984. Both are cited by p02 for this very proposition.
- **Also:** Sierpiński 1915 (cited too) predates Hausdorff dimension (Hausdorff 1918/19) and concerns only the gasket.
- **Correction:** d = log(p(p+1)/2)/log p; for p = 3, log 6/log 3 ≈ 1.6309.

### P2. MAJOR, VERIFIED: "the mod-3 case gives the strictly smaller dimension" is false, and "three copies" is wrong.
- **Where:** p02:114 "The mod-3 case gives the strictly smaller dimension log 4 / log 3 ≈ 1.262"; p02:91 "Three smaller copies of the whole triangle appear at the base level. Each of those contains three smaller copies"; figure caption p02:176 "First 27 rows showing three self-similar copies at scale p=3"; same in the figure panel "Three self-similar copies emerge at scale p = 3".
- **Dimensions:** 1.6309 (mod 3) > 1.5850 (mod 2).
- **Copies:** Rows 0..3^n−1 contain 1 + 2 + 3 = 6 upright copies of the 3^{n−1}-row triangle. `p02_pascal.py` finds 6 copies in the first 27 rows. "Three copies at scale 1/3" would mean dimension log 3/log 3 = 1, so the text gives three mutually inconsistent copy counts: 3 (prose), 4 (the formula and the Lean `(p+1)^m`), and the true 6.
- **Figure inset:** The figure says "Hausdorff dimension d = log 4/log 3 ≈ 1.585". log 4/log 3 is 1.262; 1.585 is log 3/log 2. A second, minor mislabel: the mod-2 panel caption says "Odd entries (gold)" but the plotted points are dark blue.
- **Correction:** "Mod 3 has the larger dimension log 6/log 3 ≈ 1.631; six copies at each scale."

### P3. CRITICAL, VERIFIED: nothing in the mod-p fractal singles out the prime 3.
- **Where:** p02:123 "why the prime 3 is singled out … why not 5, or 7 … will find the answer in Proposition 4.2 and in the ramification of 3 in Z[ω]: these are two faces of the same structure"; also abstract p02:31.
- **Why it fails:**
  - Proposition 4.2 and Lucas's theorem hold uniformly for every prime p, so they cannot distinguish 3.
  - Every prime ramifies in some quadratic field (5 in Q(√5), 7 in Q(√−7)).
  - No mathematical relation between Lucas mod 3 and the ramification of 3 in Z[ω] is given, here or in the cited p05 Remark 2.4.
- **Correction:** Remove the claim.

### P4. MAJOR, VERIFIED (sources): Khayyam's surviving algebra does not contain the triangle, and "first documented" is false.
- **Where:** p02:71 "Khayyam described the triangle in his Maqālah fī al-jabr wa al-muqābalah … around 1070 CE. We know this not only from the manuscript itself …"; p02:29, 46, 155 ("first documented it", "This is not disputed").
- **What the treatise is:** Khayyam's algebra (Risāla fī'l-barāhīn ʿalā masāʾil al-jabr wa'l-muqābala) was written c. 1070 in Samarkand, under Abū Ṭāhir, not in Nishapur as bib p02:183 says. It is a classification of cubic equations solved by conic sections.
- **Where the triangle presumably was:** The root-extraction work (Mushkilāt al-ḥisāb) that presumably used binomial coefficients is lost (MacTutor; DSB). So there is no extant Khayyam manuscript of the triangle.
- **Earlier sources the table omits (p02:56–68), contradicting "the historical record is not ambiguous":**
  - Piṅgala's Meru-prastāra, described explicitly by Halāyudha (10th c.)
  - al-Karajī (953–1029), via al-Samawʾal's al-Bāhir (1144)
  - Jia Xian (c. 1050), whom Yang Hui (1261) himself credits, so "Yang Hui arrived independently" is misleading
  - Jordanus de Nemore (13th c.) in Europe
- **Correction:** Say the triangle is associated with Khayyam through a lost work on root extraction, and list the earlier sources.

### P5. MAJOR: the al-Ṭūsī citation is the wrong work. Status: SUSPECT for "cited Khayyam by name".
- **Where:** p02:71 "Naṣīr al-Dīn al-Ṭūsī, who cited Khayyam by name in 1265 [altusi1265]"; bib p02:186 "Sharḥ al-ishārāt, 1265".
- **The cited work:** Sharḥ al-Ishārāt is al-Ṭūsī's commentary on Avicenna's philosophical al-Ishārāt wa-l-tanbīhāt. It is not a mathematical text, and it is not dated 1265 (VERIFIED).
- **The right work:** al-Ṭūsī's root-extraction and binomial-coefficient material is in Jawāmiʿ al-ḥisāb bi-l-takht wa-l-turāb (1265). I found no source saying it names Khayyam (SUSPECT).
- **Correction:** Cite Jawāmiʿ al-ḥisāb, and verify the attribution claim or drop it.

### P6. MAJOR, VERIFIED: the solar-year numbers are wrong and the source is doubtful.
- **Where:** p02:83 "Khayyam calculated the solar year as 365.24219858156 days. Modern atomic-clock measurements give 365.242190 days: a discrepancy of 0.741312 seconds per year"; p03:652 "His calendar was accurate to within a second per year".
- **Problems:**
  - **Arithmetic:** (365.24219858156 − 365.242190) × 86400 = 0.741447 s, not 0.741312 s (`p02_hist.py`).
  - **Provenance:** The 11-decimal figure equals 365 + 683/2820 = 365.2421985815603 to every printed digit. That is the 2820-year cycle of a 20th-century proposal, so it is very likely a modern back-calculation rather than Khayyam's measurement (Heydari-Malayeri, "A concise review of the Iranian calendar", astro-ph/0409620, raises exactly this).
  - **Wrong comparison year:** The mean tropical year in 1079 was about 365.242246 d (Laskar polynomial), so even taken at face value the discrepancy for Khayyam's epoch is about 4.1 s/yr, not 0.74.
  - **Atomic clocks:** The tropical year is an ephemeris quantity, not an "atomic-clock measurement".
- **Correction:** Drop the 11-digit figure and the 0.74 s claim, or present them as an attributed figure of doubtful authenticity.

### P7. MINOR, VERIFIED: dates are wrong or inconsistent.
- p02:46 "approximately 584 years before Pascal was born": Pascal was born in 1623, and 1623 − 1070 = 553. The figure 584 = 1654 − 1070 refers to the Traité, not Pascal's birth.

### P8. MAJOR, VERIFIED: "the fractal waited three centuries before anyone noticed it" is false and contradicts the paper's own citation.
- **Where:** p02:127 "The fractal therefore sat inside 'Pascal's Triangle' for over three centuries before anyone noticed it"; p02:129–131 (counting questions "cannot see the fractal").
- **Why:**
  - Lucas 1878, which the paper itself cites as establishing the fractal (p02:93), characterises the nonzero pattern exactly.
  - Kummer 1852 gave the p-adic valuation of binomial coefficients.
  - Glaisher 1899 counted the odd entries in each row (2^{s(n)}).
  - Fine 1947 (Amer. Math. Monthly 54) proved the nonzero count mod p in row n is ∏(n_i+1).
  - These are exactly the "how many?" questions the paper says cannot see the fractal.
- **Correction:** Remove the claim.

### P10. MINOR, VERIFIED: Khayyam did not solve cubics only with a parabola and a circle.

## C. p03: Spherical Cow Philosophy

### Q1. CRITICAL, VERIFIED: the "five forced consequences" are not forced.
- **Where:** p03:382–416.
- **Item by item:**
  - **(1) Cascade structure:** The text says "By the Chinese Remainder Theorem, gcd(3^K, ℓ) = 1": the gcd is trivial and it is the premise of the CRT, not a consequence. Nothing about twin primes "forces" 3^K. All 3^{K−1} lifts of 2 (mod 3) are admissible and equidistributed (X3).
  - **(3) Three-level filtration:** It has 2K levels (X3). "Triply-well-factorable" is a property one imposes on sieve weights, not something forced by Z[ω]; Pascadi's triply-well-factorable weights are for general moduli (fact 4).
  - **(4) θ_W = 5/8:** see X1 and Q4.
  - **(5) Combinatorial origin:** see X2.
- **The closing claim is false by the paper's own text:** "None requires additional hypotheses" (p03:416) contradicts item (4), which needs "Condition W3 verified in [WakilW3]".

### Q2. MAJOR, VERIFIED: the classical literature is misrepresented (facts 5 and 7).
- **Where and what is wrong:**
  - **p03:229–230** "Classical Bombieri–Vinogradov gives θ = 1/2 for *all* moduli": BV is an average over q ≤ x^{1/2}(log x)^{−B}, not a statement for each modulus. GRH does not exceed 1/2 for a single modulus.
  - **p03:67–68** "the θ = 1/2 ceiling for twin primes over two centuries of effort": the level-of-distribution notion and BV date from 1965, and the twin prime conjecture from about 1849. And 1/2 is not a ceiling for well-factorable weights: Bombieri–Friedlander–Iwaniec 4/7 (1986), Zhang, Maynard 3/5, Lichtman, Pascadi 5/8.
  - **p03:235–237 and 252–262** "Classical methods … cannot exploit structure specific to a particular prime", and Kloosterman cancellation in a cyclic group being "invisible from the universal vantage point": the prime-power-modulus literature does exactly this, using the cyclic and p-adic structure of (Z/p^n)^*. Examples are Postnikov; Barban–Linnik–Tshudakov, x^{3/8}; Gallagher, 2/5; Huxley, 5/12; Banks–Shparlinski, about 0.47. All those exponents are below 1/2, the opposite of 5/8.
  - **p03:231–233:** Maynard–Tao need only θ = 1/2 for bounded gaps, so the "θ = 1/2 barrier" is not the obstruction (see X4).
- **Correction:** Restate BV as an average result and cite the beyond-1/2 and prime-power literature correctly.

### Q3. MAJOR, VERIFIED: the Sophie Germain "non-instance" is incoherent and cannot have been "computationally confirmed".
- **Where:** p03:80–82, 172–174, 488–511 (Prop 5.1, Remark 5.2), 632–634.
- **Problems:**
  1. **The coupling argument applies equally to twin primes.** "The mod-3 residue of 2p+1 is determined by that of p" is just as true of p+2. In both cases the partner is an affine function of p, so its residue mod 3^K is determined by p's.
  2. **Identical local structure (`p03_twin_sg.py`).**
     - Mod 3 both forbid {0,1}.
     - Mod 2 both forbid {0}.
     - Mod every odd ℓ checked (5, 7, 11, 13) both forbid exactly 2 classes.
     - The Hardy–Littlewood constant is the same 2C₂. Predictions match counts to 10^8: twin 440,368 predicted vs 440,312 actual; SG 423,294 vs 423,140.
     - SG primes are also uniform over lifts mod 9, 27 and 81 (χ² 0.1/5.1/9.0).
     - No 3-adic difference from twins exists, so "do not exhibit the cascade moduli enhancement" distinguishes nothing.
  3. **Category error.** A level of distribution is a property of primes in arithmetic progressions, the input to GPY/Maynard. It is the same input for every admissible tuple ({n, n+2} or {n, 2n+1}), so "SG at 1/2, twins at 5/8" is not a meaningful contrast.
  4. **The k value is inconsistent and the "prediction" cannot fail.**
     - p03 says k = 2 by mod-3 coupling.
     - p01 Prop "Sophie Germain non-instance" says k = 1 by mod-2 coupling.
     - The P03 Lean header says "effective k=1".
     - Since θ₁ = θ₂ = 1/2 = the BV value, the "prediction" coincides with the default and cannot fail.
  5. **Nothing was confirmed.** A level of distribution is an asymptotic statement (for all A, all q ≤ x^{θ−ε}), so finite computation cannot confirm it. The cited source (p06 Thm 7.1) has no proof and cites itself as "[?, Theorem 7.1]".
- **Correction:** Withdraw the non-instance as evidence.

### Q4. MAJOR, VERIFIED: the 2/3 ceiling is said to be "improved" to the smaller 5/8.
- **Where:** p03:400–404 "Three-fold Cauchy–Schwarz over the filtration gives ceiling θ ≤ 2/3; cyclic group cancellation … improves this to θ_W = 5/8".
- Since 5/8 = 0.625 < 2/3 ≈ 0.667, an "improvement" from 2/3 to 5/8 is a weaker exponent, so the description is self-contradictory.
- In any case, by X1 the stated theorem holds for every θ < 1, so neither 2/3 nor 5/8 is a ceiling.

### Q5. MAJOR, VERIFIED: the Pascadi citation is misattributed (fact 4).
- **Where:** bib p03:721–724 "A. Pascadi, Level of distribution 5/8 for smooth moduli, arXiv:2505.00653v2"; used at p03:430 as an avenue for "combining cascade moduli with smooth moduli".
- The actual title is "On the exponents of distribution of primes and smooth numbers". It concerns primes with triply-well-factorable weights and smooth numbers to general moduli up to x^{5/8−o(1)}, with nothing about 3^K.
- 3^K are themselves 3-smooth, but X1 shows this family adds nothing.

### Q6. MAJOR, VERIFIED: the "same pattern" as Shannon's AEP is the opposite pattern.
- **Where:** p03:440–457 "Both are instances of the same pattern as the cascade moduli result: a structural constraint forces an exponential space to concentrate onto a subspace".
- In the AEP the typical set is a vanishing fraction of the space: |A_ε^{(n)}|/|𝒜|^n ≤ 2^{−n(log|𝒜| − H − ε)} → 0.
- In the θ_k scheme the valid fraction is 1 − k/2^k → 1. The constraint removes a vanishing fraction.
- So the claimed structural identity fails.

### Q7. MAJOR, VERIFIED: the information theory about degraded channels is backwards.
- **Where:** p03:505–506 "a degraded channel has lower capacity because input-output dependence increases".
- Capacity is C = max I(X;Y). For a degraded channel X→Y→Z, the data-processing inequality gives I(X;Z) ≤ I(X;Y). Capacity drops because input–output dependence (mutual information) decreases.

### Q8. MAJOR, VERIFIED: the non-circularity and "guarantee" arguments fail.
- **Where:** p03:482–486 "The derivation could in principle have produced a different value of θ … The fact that it produces 5/8 > 1/2 is a non-trivial output"; p03:460–463 "This order … guarantees that neither result was constructed to match the other".
- 5/8 is an input: k = 3 is chosen (p04 Prop 2.4 picks j = 2, 4, 6), and the stated theorem holds for every θ < 1 (X1).
- The value coincides with Pascadi's general-modulus 5/8 of May 2025, which predates these papers, so "guarantees" is unwarranted.

### L1. MAJOR, VERIFIED: P02 `ternary_survivor_count` is a tautology with a false docstring.
- **Where:** P02_KhayyamsTriangle.lean:81–85 "At scale 3^K, survivor count is 4^K".

### L2. MAJOR, VERIFIED: P02 encodes the wrong dimension formula, and the "mod 3 smaller" axiom asserts a falsehood about the real fractals.
