# p01 — Constitutional Forcing (lead reviewer)

Checks script: audit/scripts/p01/p01_checks.py (all numbers below reproduced by it).

- P01-1 CRITICAL VERIFIED — Counting Theorem is not a proof (l.242–270, 609–611).
  Under (CF1) (c_i = 1 iff constraint i holds) and l.262 ("failing any constraint is sufficient for invalidity"), one failing constraint invalidates 2^{k-1} configurations; invalid = 2^k − 1, valid = 1 for every k (k=3: 7 invalid, not 3). The induction step drops the fact that the k−1 invalid configurations at depth k−1 each split into two at depth k. "Exactly k invalid" holds only because (C3) stipulates it, and (C3) contradicts (CF1) for k ≥ 2. θ_k = (2^k−k)/2^k is a definition, not a theorem; l.609–611 "the formula is a theorem, not a fit" is false.

- P01-2 CRITICAL VERIFIED — Kolmogorov α = 3/4 (l.83–85, 108, 128, 406–456, 521, 635–636).
  (a) θ_2 = (2^2−2)/2^2 = 1/2 (the paper's own Table 1, l.283); l.438 instead computes (2^3−2)/2^3.
  (b) Remark l.450–455 ("appropriate base space") makes the framework unfalsifiable: 3/4 = (2^2−1)/2^2 = (2^3−2)/2^3 = (2^4−4)/2^4.
  (c) The stated bound |ω|∞ ≤ C ε^{3/4}/ν^{3/4} is dimensionally inconsistent: ε^{3/4}/ν^{3/4} has units T^{-3/2}, vorticity has T^{-1}. K41's vorticity scale is (ε/ν)^{1/2}.
  (d) The genuine K41 "3/4" is the Kolmogorov length η = (ν³/ε)^{1/4}, i.e. η/L ~ Re^{-3/4}. It was obtained a priori by dimensional analysis in 1941, so "84 years … lacking an a priori derivation" is false. It is not called a "dissipation exponent α". Measured dissipation scales also deviate from it at higher order (intermittency).
  (e) "Helicity orthogonality … forbidding the resonant coupling necessary for finite-time blow-up" asserts Navier–Stokes regularity, which is the open Millennium problem.
  (f) Independence (C2) fails. The helical decomposition u = u⁺ + u⁻ is defined only for solenoidal (∇·u = 0) fields, so constraint 2 presupposes constraint 1.
  (g) The "8 = 2^3 triadic configurations" likely comes from Waleffe's (1992) eight helicity-sign classes of a triad (s_k, s_p, s_q) ∈ {±}^3, not from "three couplings present/absent" (SUSPECT about the origin).
  Fix: delete the instance throughout (abstract, §3.4, remark, conjecture remark, acknowledgements, website, essays, cover letter, registry).

- P01-3 CRITICAL VERIFIED — Cascade-moduli instance (l.334–371).
  (a) The three "levels" are not independent. For p > 3 the admissible residues of twin primes mod 9, 27 and 81 are exactly the lifts of p ≡ 2 (mod 3), with counts 3, 9 and 27. Primes below 10^6 confirm this: residues {2,5,8} mod 9 and all 9 lifts mod 27. Under the paper's own (C2) the depth is 1, which would predict 1/2, not 5/8.
  (b) The argument mixes twin-prime admissibility with the level of distribution of primes to moduli 3^K. These are different objects.
  (c) No "eight configuration classes" are ever defined.
  (d) For a single modulus q, even GRH gives only q ≤ x^{1/2−ε}. The known results for prime-power moduli are Barban–Linnik–Tshudakov 3/8, Gallagher 2/5, Huxley 5/12 and Banks–Shparlinski ≈0.47. A level of 5/8 for q = 3^K would go beyond GRH, so it cannot be cited as an established instance.

- P01-4 MAJOR VERIFIED — Pascadi misattributed (l.108–110, 129–130, 329–331, 656–659).
  arXiv:2505.00653 is titled "On the exponents of distribution of primes and smooth numbers", not "Level of distribution 5/8 for smooth moduli". It gives x^{5/8−o(1)} for smooth numbers, and for primes with triply-well-factorable weights, over general moduli. The method is large-sieve inequalities for exceptional Maass forms plus Kim–Sarnak 7/64, and Lichtman already had 5/8 conditional on Selberg's eigenvalue conjecture. Pascadi does not "restrict to cascade moduli" or "increase depth to k = 3" (l.329–331).

- P01-5 MAJOR VERIFIED — Bombieri–Vinogradov instance (l.302–332).
  BV is an average over q ≤ x^{1/2}(log x)^{-B}, not equidistribution for each q ≤ x^{1/2} (l.305). (CF4) l.325–327 is wrong: GRH does not lift the level above 1/2, and Elliott–Halberstam is a separate conjecture. Levels above 1/2 are already known for well-factorable weights without any 3^K restriction: BFI 4/7, Zhang, Maynard 3/5, Lichtman 66/107, Pascadi 5/8. None of 4/7, 3/5 or 66/107 has the form (2^k−k)/2^k, which contradicts the "any improvement requires increasing depth" uniqueness claim. Calling "primality is binary" a constitutional constraint would assign 1/2 to any predicate.

- P01-6 MAJOR VERIFIED — Shannon instance (l.373–404).
  (CF3) sets θ_1 = H/log|A|, which is not 1/2. Bernoulli(0.1) gives 0.469 and Bernoulli(0.25) gives 0.811. The typical set's share of A^n is ≈ 2^{-n(log|A|−H)} → 0, not H/log|A|. Capacity C (e.g. a BSC's 1 − h(p)) is not 1/2 either. So the instance contradicts Table 1 rather than confirming it.

- P01-7 MAJOR VERIFIED — Sophie Germain "non-instance" and falsifiability (l.191–200, 466–488, 612–614).
  (a) "2p+1 is odd" holds for every integer p; it is not a constraint. The real coupling is mod 3: every SG prime p > 3 satisfies p ≡ 2 (mod 3), all 7,744 below 10^6. That is the same mod-3 condition the paper counts as twin-prime level 1.
  (b) θ_1 = θ_2 = 1/2, so depth 1 versus depth 2 cannot be told apart. The "prediction confirmed computationally" therefore tests nothing, and no data or definition of the "level of distribution for Sophie Germain pairs" is given.
  (c) The remark at l.195–199 is self-contradictory ("θ = 1/2, not the naïve θ_2 = 1/2").

- P01-8 MAJOR VERIFIED — Status overclaims (l.76–85, 127–130, 520–522, 634–636).
  The paper says "three proven instances", with "the prime arithmetic instance … established 2025–2026" and Kolmogorov counted as a k = 2 instance. After P01-2 to P01-6, no instance satisfies the paper's own definition. The k = 1 entries rest on a vacuous binary predicate, and k = 2 and k = 3 fail on independence.

- P01-9 MINOR VERIFIED — Programme numbering (l.93–95, 538–592).
  The paper describes a "seven-paper program" numbered Paper 00–06 and calls itself Paper 00. The repo has p01–p09, the website labels it PAPER 01, and the registry skill numbers p05/p06/p09 differently again. The program table also assigns k = 3 to Khayyam's Triangle and k = 1,3 to Shannon–Wakil without derivation.

- P01-10 MINOR — l.108: e and φ are listed as "forced" constants but are never treated. Remove, or treat them.
- P01-11 MINOR VERIFIED — l.183–200 vs l.466–488: the Sophie Germain example is described as "two modular conditions" in one place and as mod-2 parity conditions in the other. It should be stated consistently, and correctly (mod 3).
